# Third-party material

- **AIReg-Bench** (Marino, Hunter, Jamali et al., 2025, arXiv:2510.01474), dataset at
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/camlsys/AIReg-Bench, licensed **CC-BY-4.0**. The Screener's example documents
  (`screener/samples/samples.json`) are adapted from it: excerpts combined into multi-section files, and one with an
  added reviewer note. The evaluation code reads the upstream benchmark from a local clone (`external/aireg-bench`).
- **EU AI Act text** (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), Articles 9, 10, 12, 14 and 15 as extracted by AIReg-Bench
  (`screener/articles.txt`). EU legislative texts may be reused under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU.
- Models: NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano and Nemotron 3 Ultra, served by Nebius Token Factory, under their providers'
  terms.
