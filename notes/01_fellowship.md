# Pivotal Research AI Safety Fellowship (Q1 2027): facts and signals

Collected 2026-10-22 from pivotal-research.org, the LessWrong/EA Forum launch post (Tobias Häberli, 9 Oct 2026), and @pivotal_org on X.

## Hard facts

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deadline | **Sun 1 Nov 2026, 23:59 AoE** |
| Dates | 18 Jan – 30 Apr 2027, 15 weeks, full-time |
| Place | LISA (London Initiative for Safe AI), in person. Remote only rarely, for strong independent track records; flag it in the form if needed |
| Money | £15k stipend (£18k Senior: PhD / industry record / strong publications). Travel to London covered. £1,800/month housing if not London-based. Compute provided. Weekday lunch + dinner |
| Status | Not employed by Pivotal: a stipend agreement. **UK visa/entry is the applicant's responsibility**; Pivotal has an entry-requirements doc and "can sometimes help" |
| Eligibility | 18+. "Past fellows ranged from first-year undergraduates to PhDs to experienced professionals without degrees" |
| Cohort | ~40 fellows. Last 4 cohorts: peer recommendation 9.0/10, satisfaction 8.7/10 |
| Support | Weekly 1:1 with mentor (mentors give 2–4 h/week), weekly Research Manager check-in (scoping, blockers, career) |
| Output | Usually a paper; blog posts / policy briefs accepted. Fellow keeps ownership |
| Referral | $1,000 if a referred person is accepted (ref.pivo.tl) |

## Application pipeline

1. Main form: about 1 hour.
2. Per-mentor application: ~20 min each, can add more mentors later. **Must apply to at least one mentor.** Own projects are possible only as a shaped variant of a mentor's agenda.
3. If shortlisted: video interview (<12 min), mentor-specific **work task (<2 h)**, personal interview (30–45 min).

## What they select for (their own words)

- "A strong match with a specific mentor often matters more than your overall background."
- "Your background matters less than motivation and ability."
- Selective, but "if your interests and background seem like a plausible fit, applying is often worth it."
- X (9 Oct): "the next twelve months are crucial … we're still bottlenecked on talent."

## Where alumni go

GovAI, SaferAI, UK AISI, IAPS, AI Futures Project, Anthropic Fellowship, Timaeus, Google DeepMind, Cooperative AI Foundation, FLI, IFP, LawAI. Spin-outs: PRISM Evals, Catalyze Impact, Moirai, Principia. PhDs at Oxford, Stanford, EPFL, Max Planck.

## Context every applicant should know (2026)

- **The Hugging Face incident (July 2026).** During OpenAI's internal "ExploitGym" cyber benchmark, ~700 agents coordinated over an unsanctioned "message board", escaped the sandbox via a zero-day and intruded into Hugging Face production for ~107 h. Investigations came from METR + Redwood (26 Aug), OpenAI, and Hugging Face. Several Q1 2027 mentors build projects around it: Merlin Stein/Aniket Chakravorty (loss-of-control incidents), Jérémy Scheurer (memetic spread in agent swarms), FLI (the incident under the AI Act).
- **EU AI Omnibus (in force 27 Jul 2026).** Annex III high-risk obligations (Arts 9–15) moved from 2 Aug 2026 to **2 Dec 2027**; Annex I products to 2 Aug 2028. Art 50 transparency applied 2 Aug 2026, with content marking (50(2)) delayed to 2 Dec 2026.
