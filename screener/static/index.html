AuditBench Screener

Pre-screen AI-system technical documentation against EU AI Act Articles 9, 10, 12, 14 and 15, the high-risk requirements that apply from 2 December 2027. Each Article section is judged on its own, sampled three times by NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano and escalated to Nemotron 3 Ultra when the samples disagree, all on Nebius Token Factory. This tool helps a human reviewer: it does not give legal advice.

Results appear here, one card per Article.
Method and evaluation: github.com/Rcidshacker/auditbench. Examples adapted from AIReg-Bench (Marino et al., 2025, CC-BY-4.0).
